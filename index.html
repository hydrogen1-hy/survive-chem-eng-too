Welcome

These are calculators for undergraduate chemical engineering. Use them to check your answers and to see how a problem is solved: each one shows the equations with your numbers in them, and the mass balance, energy balance and calculus pages write out their solutions step by step, the way you would on paper.

Every number on this site is worked out by ordinary code running in your browser: formulas, tables of constants and equation solvers. No AI produces any answer, nothing is sent to a server, and the same inputs always give the same result. The maths is tested against hand-solved problems.

Available now

  • Mass balances Draw a flowsheet with mixers, splitters, separators and reactors, including recycle and purge. It does the degrees-of-freedom analysis, tells you what is missing or contradictory, solves for everything else, and writes the answers on your drawing.
  • Energy balances Heat capacities and sensible heat, phase changes, heats of reaction, adiabatic flame temperature and the steady-flow balance, with the working shown. The mass balance flowsheet can carry temperatures and heat duties too.
  • Integrals and derivatives Integrals and derivatives, including partial derivatives, worked step by step with the rule named at each step. Every integral is checked by differentiating the answer, and when no method applies it says so instead of guessing.
  • Double and triple integrals Cartesian, polar, cylindrical and spherical coordinates with the Jacobian, one worked integral per variable, a picture of the region, and a button that swaps the order of integration.
  • Electrochemistry Faraday’s law with current efficiency, cell potentials from the Nernst equation, free energy, and electrical energy per kg of product.
  • Vapour pressure The Antoine equation for common solvents, or with your own constants. Pressure from temperature, or boiling point from pressure, with a plot.
  • Ideal gas PV = nRT. Solve for whichever of P, V, n or T you’re missing.
  • Unit converter Pressure, energy, power, flow rates, viscosity, density, temperature, time, current, charge and more.
