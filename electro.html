Electrochemistry

Faraday’s law with current efficiency, cell potentials from the Nernst equation, and the electrical energy it costs to make a product. Every result shows the equation with your numbers in it.

Product

Pick what is being made, or enter your own molar mass and electrons per mole. The first and third calculators below use this.

Faraday’s law

m = I · t · η · M / (z · F). Fill in three, solve for the fourth. η is the current (Faradaic) efficiency: the fraction of the charge that goes into the product you want.

Cell voltage

Pick the reduction half-reaction at the cathode and the one at the anode (it runs in reverse there). E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode, then Nernst for your conditions.

Potentials are standard values at 25 °C versus the standard hydrogen electrode. They are typical textbook values and your course table may differ in the last digit, so check it before relying on a result. Choose “Custom” to enter your own.

Energy consumption

Electrical energy per kg of product: z · F · V / (3600 · M · η). Uses the product above.
