All calculators

Grouped by course. Every one shows the equation with your numbers in it. Courses that don’t have a calculator yet are on the roadmap.

Process engineering

  • Mass balances Draw a flowsheet with mixers, splitters, separators and reactors, recycle and purge. Degrees-of-freedom analysis, redundant and contradictory specifications, a step-by-step solution, and the answers on your drawing.
  • Energy balances Sensible heat, phase changes, heats of reaction and combustion, adiabatic reactor and flame temperatures, and the steady-flow energy balance, each with its working shown and an editable data table. The mass balance flowsheet can carry the energy balance too: stream temperatures, unit heat duties, and the energy steps.
  • Electrochemistry Faraday’s law and current efficiency, Nernst equation and cell potential, ΔG and equilibrium constant, energy per kg of product.
  • Vapour pressure Antoine equation: vapour pressure from temperature or boiling point from pressure, with a plot.
  • Ideal gas PV = nRT, solving for any one of the four quantities.

Calculus 3

  • Integrals and derivatives Indefinite, definite and improper integrals, and derivatives including partial derivatives, worked step by step with the rule named at each step, and a plot. Every integral is checked by differentiating the answer; when no method applies it says so and gives a clearly marked numeric value instead of guessing.
  • Double and triple integrals Write the limits innermost first and get one integral per variable with its working, a picture of the region or solid, and the answer checked against a separate numerical integration. Polar, cylindrical and spherical coordinates with the Jacobian, and a button that swaps the order of integration.

General

  • Unit converter Pressure, energy, power, mass, length, volume, flow rates, density, viscosity, temperature, time, current, charge, current density.
